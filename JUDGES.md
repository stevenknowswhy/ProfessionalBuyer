# Judges Dossier (condensed)

Build Personal Agents Hack, Oct 4 2026.
Sources: [research/judges-sources.md](research/judges-sources.md).

## 1. Nikita Shamgunov — VP Eng, Databricks (ex-CEO, Neon)

- Company: Databricks — 2026 story is the *agentic data stack* (Agent Bricks, Genie).
  Neon (acquired May 2025) is the #1 co-host; the top prize is Neon AI Gateway credits.
- Passions: Postgres-backed agents; memory on governed data; "agents read, loop, and write
  differently from software built for people."
- Catches his eye: an agent that *learns your data* — purchase history in Postgres that compounds.

## 2. Scott Johnston — CEO, Fly.io (ex-CEO, Docker)

- Company: Fly.io — 2026 pivot to "Computers for Agents"; $25M Series D; Sprites agent sandboxes
  ("instant, cheap when idle, persistent when needed").
- Passions: long-running, stateful compute as a first-class primitive.
- Catches his eye: price *watches* running for weeks on Sprites — a real computer behind the agent,
  not a laptop demo.

## 3. Abhi Aiyer — Co-founder/CTO, Mastra

- Company: Mastra — TypeScript agent framework; Observational Memory; Harness primitive;
  one-line runtime governance (OpenBox); weekly "AI Agents Hour."
- Passions: observability, memory that survives sessions, governance that isn't bolted on.
- Catches his eye: build on Mastra; a live trace side-panel; approval gates where money moves.

## 4. Jeff Wang — Co-founder, Exa

- Company: Exa — "the search engine for AI"; $250M at $2.5B (a16z-led, May 2026).
  "AI will search the web more than humans."
- Passions: perfect search; AI-native web access; fresh data over SEO spam.
- Catches his eye: the Buyer *is* his thesis — an agent living on real-time web price data via Exa.

## 5. Catherine Jue — Co-founder/CEO, Kernel (YC S25, $22M)

- Company: Kernel — browser infra for agents: sandboxed Chromium, stealth, session replays,
  live view, "turn any agent into an HTTPS endpoint."
- Passions: agents that use the internet like humans; credential safety; visible replays.
- Catches his eye: **use Kernel, not raw Puppeteer** — verify prices and check out on real sites
  with the live view on screen during the demo.

## 6. Rhys Sullivan — Founder, Executor (YC, open source)

- Company: Executor — MCP integration layer; per-tool allow/approve/block policies;
  generative UI rendered inside agents.
- Passions: integrations without glue code; UI generated in the agent's execution context.
- Catches his eye: price-comparison *cards* rendered as generative UI; approval policies on
  the buy tool.

## 7. Simon Farshid — Founder, assistant-ui (YC, open source)

- Company: assistant-ui — OSS React agent-chat library (~1.4M npm downloads/mo).
  "The hard part is the state." **Judges Best UI side quest ($750).**
- Passions: human↔agent collaboration UX; streaming, interruption, edit-and-rerun, approvals.
- Catches his eye: a genuinely polished interface — and note every rival ships a chat UI,
  so a *savings dashboard* surface differentiates.

## 8. Haakam Aujla — Co-founder/CEO, AgentMail (YC S25, $6M seed)

- Company: AgentMail — email inboxes for agents; "the next billion users will be AI agents."
- Passions: agents as first-class email citizens; async collaboration over email.
- Catches his eye: receipt mining through the agent's own inbox; the daily briefing email.
  This is the most on-brief co-host product for the Buyer.

## 9. Erik Thorelli — Head of DX, CodeRabbit

- Company: CodeRabbit — AI code review; free for OSS; $1M pledged to OSS maintainers in 2026.
  **Judges Best Open Source side quest ($10k credits).**
- Passions: developer experience; open source as a public good.
- Catches his eye: ship the project as **public open source with a great README** —
  that alone enters you for his side quest.

## 10. Jakub Krehel — Founding Design Engineer, Interfere (ex-OpenSea)

- Company: Interfere — "self-healing layer of the internet" (incident response).
- Passions (his own writing, jakub.kr): interface craft — OKLCH color, concentric radii,
  optical alignment, motion so subtle it goes unnoticed.
- Catches his eye: production-grade polish. He is the craft conscience and the swing vote
  when the room debates which demo "felt premium." (Note: he is the *design* judge —
  bait him with UI craft, not retry logic.)

## 11. James da Costa — Partner, a16z (AI Apps)

- Background: fintech/B2B investor; Fingo (YC digital bank) co-founder; *Fintech Wars* author;
  Stanford HAI researcher.
- Passions: products that could be companies; commercial instinct; proof of work.
- Catches his eye: the Buyer is company-shaped (commerce/fintech wedge). His question —
  spoken or not — is always "is it a company?" Have an answer: wedge, users, monetization.
